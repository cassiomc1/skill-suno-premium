# Emotional, Compositional & Spatial DNA Architecture

Use this reference when crafting tracks that require nuanced emotional contrast, cinematic tension-and-release, deep electronic groove psychology, or section-by-section instrumentation choreography.

## 2. The 5 Intensity Tiers

| Intensity | Delivery Modifier | Dynamic Philosophy | Key Songwriting Directive |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `subtle` | `whispered intimate` | Restrained, close-mic, felt beneath the surface | Keep dynamics delicate; let emotion live in melody and imagery rather than volume. |
| `building` | `building emotion` | Slow-burn arc from restraint to emotional peak | Start intimate in Verse 1; progressively add layers so the Final Chorus hits hardest. |
| `peak` | `strong expressive` | Confident, expansive, high-energy payoff | Deliver strong dynamic projection while preserving clarity—avoid melodrama. |
| `raw` | `raw expressive` | Visceral, unguarded, sharp quiet-to-loud contrasts | Allow trembling/exposed breaks in the bridge; keep phrasing grounded and real. |
| `dopamine` | `euphoric addictive energy` | Maximum anticipation-gap → release payoff | Engineer stark contrast: quiet/intimate verse → rising pre-chorus → 1-beat silence gap → explosive escalated chorus/drop. |

> **Electronic Groove Rule**: When writing melancholic, raw, or subtle electronic dance music, keep the rhythmic pulse intact—let the sadness or tension live in harmony, melody, and lyrics rather than killing the tempo.

## 4. Compositional Grammar Principles

Choose the grammar that matches the track's aesthetic rather than applying maximum loudness everywhere:

1. **Anti-Escalation** *(best for hypnotic verses & bridges)*: Plateau as peak—maintain hypnotic tension and quietly redirect apparent builds before release. (Reserve actual payoff for the chorus when a strong chorus is desired.)
2. **Evolving Loop Form** *(intro & verses)*: Each cycle shifts one subtle parameter (filter cutoff, percussion layer, delay tail) so micro-variation becomes structural movement.
3. **Negative Space** *(intro, bridge, outro, pre-drop gap)*: Silence carries equal compositional weight to sound. Pulling elements away right before a hook multiplies perceived impact.
4. **Ritual Repetition** *(hypnotic grooves)*: Use steady rhythmic/harmonic repetition as anchor while varying timbre and counterpoint above it.
5. **Fragile Dynamics** *(intimate verses, bridges, acoustic tracks)*: Quietness as power—close-mic vulnerability forces the listener to lean in.
6. **Delayed Resolution** *(bridges, bittersweet endings)*: Hold suspended 7ths/9ths or defer the tonic cadence so emotional longing lingers past the final bar.
7. **Strong Chorus Contrast** *(hook-driven tracks)*: Contrast restrained verses and sparse bridges against a wide, full-spectrum chorus with clear vocal layering.

### Contrasting Electronic DNA Archetypes
- **Melancholic / Hypnotic Electronic DNA**: Warm analog saturation, minor 7th/9th ambiguity, negative space, evolving loops, sidechain breath, bittersweet resolution without aggressive festival drops.
- **Uplifting / Peak Electronic DNA**: Rising filter sweeps, anticipation gaps before the chorus/drop, expansive synth leads, stacked harmonies or gospel-inspired choir lift, triumphant yet grounded resolution without cheesy mainstage clichés.

## 5. Section × Instrument Choreography Vocabulary

Use concrete **section-aware instrument roles** in your Style prompt and parenthesized production cues:

| Section | Bass | Pads / Chords | Keys / Melody | Drums / Groove | Vocals |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Intro** | filtered sub entering quietly | evolving stereo pad opening slowly | sparse minor 9th voicing, gentle delay | minimal kick/pulse establishing pocket | distant whisper or instrumental space |
| **Verse** | locked walking or pulsing groove | subtle breathing texture leaving center clear | restrained broken chords, intimate motif | understated groove, rimshots, tight hats | close-mic storytelling, conversational diction |
| **Chorus / Drop** | octave lift, full low-end weight | wide harmonic wash, chord stabs | expressive melodic swells, bright motif | full driving kit, open hats, snappy crack | strong hook, layered harmonies, wide chorus lift |
| **Bridge / Breakdown** | sparse low pedal or receding sub | filtered texture with silence gaps | isolated single-note motif in wide reverb | half-time feel or stripped percussion | exposed vulnerable lead, close-mic breath |
| **Final Chorus / Drop** | maximum low-end impact and drive | all harmonic layers open, ultra-wide stereo | soaring counterpoint / triumphant cascade | peak-energy fills driving forward | stacked doubles, octave harmonies, crowd/choir response, ad-libs |
| **Outro** | decelerating pulse dissolving cleanly | long atmospheric tail collapsing inward | resolving 7th arpeggio, final centered note | stripping down to final clean hit | single fading phrase before clean instrumental tail |

*(Adapt instrument names to the selected genre—for acoustic/roots/jazz/classical tracks, replace synths/sub with upright bass, strings, guitars, accordion, brass, or acoustic percussion.)*

## 6. Optional Spatial / Binaural Psychoacoustic Arc

Only include spatial/binaural production language when the user asks for **immersive headphone, 3D/8D spatial, meditative, or hypnotic electronic sound design** (keep kick and sub bass centered in mono below ~90 Hz while panning pads, delays, and textures):

| Section | Psychoacoustic Target | Spatial Movement |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Intro** | Theta (6 Hz) — relaxed anticipation | Subtle left-right sub/pad sweep |
| **Verse** | Alpha (10 Hz) — focused immersion | Gentle stereo imaging around centered vocal |
| **Chorus** | Low Beta (14 Hz) — peak engagement | Full 3D stereo spread, left-right call-and-response |
| **Bridge** | Theta (5 Hz) — deep introspection | Extreme stereo separation around intimate close-mic center |
| **Final Chorus** | Gamma (40 Hz) — transcendent lift | Full-spectrum wide immersion, synchronized harmonic bloom |
| **Outro** | Delta (2 Hz) — calm release | Stereo field gently collapsing inward into clean resolution |
